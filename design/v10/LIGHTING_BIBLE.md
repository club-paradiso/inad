# INAD v10 — Lighting bible

The booth is a real place: an immigration counter in a large terminal hall.
- **Key**: soft overhead fluorescent/LED panel, slightly cool (≈ 4500–5000 K), from above-front; gentle under-eye shadow.
- **Fill**: bounce from the counter and the examiner's monitor, weak, neutral.
- **Background**: out-of-focus terminal hall — queue posts, signage blocks, ceiling lights — dim relative to the face,
  desaturated, no readable text, no real logos or government emblems. Never a studio sweep, never a coloured gel.
- **Camera**: examiner's eye line through glass, 50–70 mm equivalent, chest-up, eyes in the upper third, slight
  downward angle allowed; modest depth of field (background soft, ears sharp).
- **Grade**: neutral, low contrast, natural skin; light glass reflection allowed at the frame edge only.
- **Forbidden**: rim light halos, teal/orange grade, beauty dish catchlights, heavy bokeh balls, golden hour.
UI match: the booth surface `--booth #111b21` sits under the portrait so a dim hall background reads as continuous.
